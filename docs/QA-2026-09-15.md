# Full QA pass — 2026-09-15

> **What this is:** an exhaustive sweep of the homepage and all four inner pages at every
> breakpoint from a 360px phone to a 2560px desktop, plus landscape phones, touch, keyboard,
> reduced motion, link health and what a crawler sees. **Nothing here has been fixed** — this is
> the survey and the plan. Issues are filed as **#99–#112** in [`AUDIT.md`](../AUDIT.md).

## Method

Against a clean production build (`rm -rf .output && npm run build`) served statically at
`localhost:5099` — never the dev server, because **headless renders card textures only on a prod
build** and the films never decode headless at all. Chrome via `playwright-core`
(`chromium.launch({ channel: 'chrome' })`); every route loaded with `?debug` for the scene hooks
(`__camDebug`, `__heroDebug`, `__probe`, `__setScroll`, `__exit*`).

| probe | what it did |
|---|---|
| **static sweep** | 5 routes × 8 sizes, at the top **and** after scrolling to the bottom: document overflow, console errors, failed requests, every link, every image, tap targets, tiny text, clipped text, duplicate ids, heading order, meta tags |
| **hit map** | a 24×24 grid of `__probe(x, y)` per viewport — where a card is actually clickable on the homepage |
| **filmstrips** | continuous wheel-scroll capture, 18 frames per chapter per size, composed into contact sheets — the whole page readable in one image |
| **collision** | 26 scroll positions × 4 chapters × 5 sizes: does any visible text run intersect the fixed chrome's rects |
| **contrast** | every visible leaf text run, measured against its **real rendered ground**: hide all text → screenshot → mean RGB per rect off a canvas → WCAG ratio, with ancestor opacity folded into the foreground. 735 samples, 440 distinct elements |
| **touch** | CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent` on a 390×844 iPhone context — swipe, tap, the top-edge return at three swipe lengths, the bottom exit, tap-to-copy read back off the real clipboard |
| **leftovers** | keyboard tab order per page, the About panel, the cash panel, In Frames' folders, reduced motion, unknown slugs, history, sound persistence |
| **links** | all ten outbound URLs by `curl` **and** in a real Chrome |

⚠️ **Two probe artefacts to know about before reading any screenshot in `scratchpad/qa/`:**
the desktop **custom cursor renders as a small ring wherever the mouse was parked** (it is not a
stray dot in the design), and **the homepage takes ~10–14s to settle** — a screenshot at 6s catches
the intro mid-flight and the composition looks wrong when it is not.

## What is healthy

Worth saying plainly, because most of it is.

- **No document overflow at any size** — 360×640 through 2560×1440, plus 844×390 and 932×430
  landscape. Nothing forces a horizontal scrollbar anywhere.
- **Zero console errors and zero page errors** on every route at every size, on load and through a
  full scroll. One benign `net::ERR_ABORTED` on a video fetch during teardown.
- **Zero failed requests, zero broken images, zero images without `alt`, zero duplicate ids, zero
  dead `#` links rendered.** `PopupCard`'s "a placeholder is not a destination" rule is holding.
- **Both exits work, on both inputs.** Bottom exit: wheel and touch, all four chapters, both sizes.
  Top-edge return: 200px and 280px thumb swipes both go home; wheel at 60/150/300/600 ms per notch
  all arrive; the two-stage staging measures as designed (first notch buys 0.02, the ring closes at
  600px, the hero only starts un-framing after that).
- **Tap-to-copy is genuinely fixed.** Verified by reading the clipboard back on a real touch
  context: `clipboard = "78…"`, `.acc-hit.ok`, label `COPIED`, live region `"… copied"`.
- **Touch navigation is sound** — swipe rotates the deck, a tap on a card opens it, the parked
  **EXPLORE** button (78×78) opens the front chapter.
- **In Frames' window is correct** — folder opens, `Escape` closes it, `GO BACK` closes it, the
  crumb and the `4 FOLDERS`/`0 ITEMS` status both track. (An earlier probe of mine reported Escape
  as broken; that was the probe — both views live in the DOM at once and `.on` switches them.)
- **With Love's cash panel is correct** — `role="dialog"`, both accounts inside, `Escape` closes.
- **The favicon is the couple's own** generated ampersand (`cu-favicon.png`) — the README and the
  tracker still call it "Milla Nova's", which is stale by about two weeks. Corrected in this pass.
- **The RSVP link resolves** (`lal.so/e/9j09CHqj2sn` → "#LaCocoVie26 — Lalso"), on all five routes,
  with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.

## Findings

Severity is about **a guest's experience of this specific site**, not a generic checklist. A wedding
site is shared as a link, read on a phone, and skimmed once.

### 🔴 #99 — A shared link has no preview, and every chapter previews as the homepage

The single highest-impact finding, because the site's entire distribution is somebody pasting the
link into WhatsApp.

`.output/public/index.html` carries exactly one `<meta>` — `viewport`. There is no `description`,
no `og:title`/`og:description`/`og:image`, no `twitter:card`, no `theme-color`, and no
`<html lang>`. With `ssr: false` the `<body>` is an empty `<div id="__nuxt">`, so a crawler or a
link-unfurler has the `<title>` and nothing else.

Worse: **all five prerendered shells carry the homepage's title.** `/us/index.html` says
*"Covenant & Uvie — A Love Story in Chapters"*, not *"Coco & Uvie — Covenant & Uvie"* — the
per-chapter title is set client-side in `app.vue` after hydration, which an unfurler never runs.

### 🔴 #100 — The bottom chrome is invisible at the top of every chapter

Measured against the real rendered ground at scroll 0, on all four chapters:

| element | 390×844 | 1440×900 |
|---|---|---|
| `WITH LOVE, C & U` | **1.03 – 1.04** | 1.57 – 1.77 |
| `OFF` (sound) | **1.08 – 1.09** | 1.28 – 4.11 |
| `RSVP` (With Love) | 1.53 | 6.47 |
| `WELCOME` (With Love / Big Day) | 2.27 / 3.13 | 6.48 / 6.28 |

AA wants 4.5. A ratio of **1.03** is the same colour twice: on The Big Day the credit measures
`fg [65,73,45]` on `bg [70,78,52]`.

The cause is asymmetry. The **top** nav flips its ink via `groundIsDark()` (AUDIT #86) and reaches
7.8–14.9 once you are on the article. The **bottom row** — the credit and the sound toggle — is
always inked in the chapter accent, and on a phone the ground beneath the hero film *is* that
accent. Same colour on same colour, on the first screen of every chapter.

⚠️ **The band itself is not the bug.** The tracker documents it deliberately: the hero card is
fitted to the viewport's **width**, so a 24×32 card on a tall phone is shorter than the frame and the
renderer's clear colour — the chapter accent — fills beneath it. That is where `READ ON` lives, and
`READ ON` is inked to be read there (6.5–9.8). What never happened is the other half: the credit and
the sound toggle sit in the same band and were never re-inked for it.

### 🔴 #101 — Page copy runs under the fixed nav, at up to 69% of scroll positions

26 scroll positions sampled per page. Counts are positions where at least one visible text run
intersects a chrome rect, and total element overlaps:

| | 360×640 | 390×844 | 768×1024 | 1440×900 | 1920×1080 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **/us** | **18/26 · 104** | 15/26 · 74 | 12/26 · 58 | 9/26 · 29 | 9/26 · 38 |
| **/with-love** | 13/26 · 37 | 12/26 · 31 | 8/26 · 16 | 10/26 · 29 | 10/26 · 28 |
| **/in-frames** | 12/26 · 27 | 5/26 · 11 | 4/26 · 9 | 6/26 · 23 | 5/26 · 21 |
| **/the-big-day** | 5/26 · 24 | 5/26 · 22 | 4/26 · 8 | 5/26 · 9 | 3/26 · 3 |

The offenders, by count: the **wordmark** (every page, every size — it is centred and so is the
copy), **WELCOME / RSVP / OFF**, the **credit**, In Frames' **"ADD YOUR PHOTOS"** popup card (which
lands on that chapter's own "See you there." and RSVP pill), and With Love's **cash dock** (which
lands on the registry marquee).

Coco & Uvie is worst because it is the couple's own words, centred, in a hand — and at 360×640 more
than two thirds of the page is read through the nav. There is no scrim: both the copy and the
chrome become mush together.

### 🟠 #102 — On desktop the centre tagline is printed across the tops of the cards

At 1024×768, 1280×800, 1440×900, 1920×1080, 2560×1440 and both landscape phones, the settled
homepage draws the front chapter's tagline **over** the two forward cards — "THOUSAND WORDS" sits
on the card edges, and at 2560 it crosses both card faces.

Portrait does not have this: `TXT_Y_MOBILE = 14` puts the tagline in the upper third and
`fitTxtMesh` scales it to the frustum. Landscape uses a fixed `TXT_Y_DESKTOP = -8` with a flat
`TXT_SCALE_DESKTOP = 0.82` and a fixed `IDLE_Y_DESKTOP` for the ring — three constants that do not
move with viewport height, which is the same failure shape as AUDIT #40, #84 and #85.

### 🟠 #103 — Landscape phone is unhandled

At 844×390 on `/us`, the hero's chapter title **"COCO & UVIE" is printed on top of the nav wordmark
"COVENANT & UVIE"**. The title band takes 30% of a 390px-tall frame, the film gets the rest and the
`focus: 1` crop leaves the couple cut off at the bottom edge, and `READ ON` is light-on-light over
the photograph. The breakpoint is width-based, so a 844×390 phone gets the desktop hero in a frame
shorter than any laptop.

### 🟠 #104 — The homepage says 44 days, The Big Day says 43

`SiteNav` computes `Math.ceil((date - now) / 86400000)`; `BigDay` computes `Math.floor(L / DAY)`
with hours and minutes beside it. They disagree on every day but an exact midnight. The two are
never on screen together — the nav countdown is `isHome`-gated — so a guest meets them one tap
apart, which is exactly enough to notice.

### 🟠 #105 — The site chrome cannot be reached by keyboard

Homepage tab order, eight presses: `body → RSVP → RSVP → body → RSVP …`. **The only focusable
control on the homepage is the RSVP link.**

`WELCOME` (opens the welcome panel), the **wordmark** (the go-home control) and the **sound toggle**
are all `<div @click>` — no `tabindex`, no `role`, no `aria-expanded`/`aria-pressed`. The About
panel does not close on `Escape` (In Frames and With Love both do), and its close control's
accessible name is the character `✕`.

Inner pages: `/the-big-day` and `/us` expose two stops (the RSVP pill and the nav RSVP);
`/in-frames` exposes its four folder buttons; `/with-love` exposes its ten registry words, one of
which is a `<button>` with no destination (Maxi Dehumidifier has no URL).

### 🟠 #106 — `prefers-reduced-motion` is ignored by everything that actually moves

Under `reducedMotion: 'reduce'` the scene still runs the full intro and keeps spinning
(`renderCount` 567–779 at the same sample points as a normal run). The media query appears in
exactly four places — `GiftAccounts`, `InFrames`, `WithLove`, `[slug].vue` — and in none of the
three things that dominate: the 3D carousel, the scroll-scrubbed chapter scenes, and the
select/return transitions.

### 🟡 #107 — Functional text that never reaches readable contrast anywhere

Best ratio each element achieved across every sampled scroll position (so these are not mid-fade
frames — an element that is ever readable is excluded):

| text | class | best CR | need |
|---|---|---|---|
| `WITH LOVE,` (8px, `opacity-40`) | `opacity-40` | 1.97 – 2.78 | 4.5 |
| `EMPTY` (In Frames folders, 8px) | `fmeta` | 2.46 | 4.5 |
| `BACK TO THE CHAPTERS` | `leave-label` | 2.39 – 2.91 | 4.5 |
| `DAYS` / `HOURS` / `MINUTES` | `u-label` | 2.65 – 2.66 | 4.5 |
| `OCTOBER 29, 2026 · LAGOS` (footer) | `end-date` | 2.68 – 3.31 | 4.5 |
| `SEND CASH INSTEAD` | `dock-note` | 2.74 | 4.5 |
| `your shots from the day` | `popup-param` | 3.44 | 4.5 |
| `#LaCocoVie26` | `hashtag` | 3.46 – 4.22 | 4.5 |
| unmarked calendar dates | `num` | 1.42 | 3 |

`BACK TO THE CHAPTERS` and the footer's own wedding date are the two that matter most — one is the
signpost out of the chapter, the other is the fact the whole site exists to carry.

⚠️ **The calendar's receded dates are deliberate** ("making 30 of its 31 numerals recede is what
stops it looking like a widget") — listed because 1.42 is far enough down that on a phone in
daylight the month reads as empty, not as ghosted.

⚠️ **The registry wall is NOT a contrast bug** and is excluded above. Its words are outline-only by
design (`color: transparent` + `-webkit-text-stroke`, filling in on `.word.on`), so a naive
measurement reports 1.0 for text that is perfectly visible. It does mean the wall disappears in
forced-colors / high-contrast mode — a known trade, noted not filed.

### 🟡 #108 — 478 KB of unreferenced poster SVGs ship on every build

`public/images/cu-p{1..4}.svg`, 119 KB each, deployed to `.output/public/images/`. Every
`CHAPTERS[].svg` points at the **`.png`** of the same name; nothing fetches the SVGs. Same class as
AUDIT #38. (`noise.png` at 792 KB is legitimately referenced but is also the single largest image
on the site, for a tiling noise texture.)

### 🟡 #109 — Tap targets below 44px on controls that matter

| control | size | where |
|---|---|---|
| `.reveal-cash` — *"or send the cash instead ↓"* | **177 × 21** | With Love, the walk-down to the accounts |
| `.cash-x` — the panel's close | **24 × 24** | With Love's cash dialog |
| `.go-back` | 88 × 29 | In Frames' empty folder |

The registry words are 26–34px tall but hundreds of pixels wide; those are inline text links in a
running wall and are exempt. `WELCOME`, `RSVP`, the account rows (172×123) and the cash dock
(352×83) are all comfortably over.

### 🟡 #110 — Dead data and stale in-file documentation in `chapterPages.js`

`POPUPS` defines seven entries that **nothing references**: `calBoth`, `dressCode`, `regFund`,
`regEspresso`, `regDinner`, `regLuggage`, `us`. Only `fullAlbum` is used. Three separate comments
describe `marks[].events[].map` and `marks[].dress` "in the-big-day below" — the-big-day has no
`marks`; its calendar section carries `monthISO`, `mark`, `kicker`, `note` and nothing else.

Same drift in the prose docs: the tracker's HANDOFF SNAPSHOT (dated 2026-09-02, the first thing a
cold pickup reads) still says the US copy is lorem ipsum, that there are two wedding days, and that
the favicon is Milla Nova's. All three were superseded. Corrected in this pass.

### 🟡 #111 — Five of the ten registry links return 403

`ng.oraimo.com` served **HTTP 403 "403 Forbidden"** for all five of its URLs — by `curl` and in a
real Chrome, product pages and the collection page alike. The other five (Fouani ×3, Taeillo, the
RSVP) return 200 with correct titles.

⚠️ **Verify from Lagos before acting.** A Nigerian storefront blocking a foreign IP is the most
likely explanation, and that would mean guests see the pages fine. If it reproduces on a Nigerian
connection, five of the ten gifts are unreachable.

### 🟡 #112 — Housekeeping

- **An unknown URL silently becomes the homepage**, HTTP 200. `/definitely-not-a-chapter` →
  `navigateTo('/')` with no message: a stale or mistyped link just lands somewhere else.
- **No `robots.txt`, no `sitemap.xml`.**
- **No `<h1>` on any page**, and no headings at all on `/` or `/the-big-day` (their titles are WebGL
  textures). With Love's first heading is an `<h3>`. Partly inherent to the medium.
- **The favicon `<link>` is emitted twice** (icon + apple-touch-icon, same file — harmless).

### 🟢 Observations, not filed

- The **US watermark numeral** (`II`/`III`) is 454px wide in a 360px viewport — clipped mid-glyph at
  the screen edge. Decorative, and no document overflow results.
- **A 120px thumb swipe at the top edge never accumulates** — five in a row each spring back. That
  is AUDIT #97's dead zone working as designed (200px arrives), but the margin is thin and this is
  the exact failure shape as AUDIT #88. Worth keeping measured.
- **The sound setting does not survive a reload.** Arguably correct — nothing should start playing
  audio on load.
- The **EXPLORE cursor paints over the About panel**, and appears mid-exit over the returning deck.
- The **About panel is 18px taller than a 390×844 viewport** with no scroll affordance; on a real
  390×760 iPhone (Safari chrome counted) the last line goes under the fold.
- **The homepage's dead centre is not on a card** at 1440×900 and 1920×1080 — the ring is turned 70°
  and the two forward cards flank the middle. Portrait and tablet portrait both centre one card
  cleanly. This reads as deliberate (it is the reference's composition) and is recorded only so the
  next person measuring a hit map is not surprised.

## Game plan

Ordered by **what a guest feels ÷ what it costs**. Nothing here is started.

### Round 1 — the link and the first screen (highest value, lowest risk)

1. **#99 — meta and share previews.** Static `description`, `og:title`/`description`/`image`/`url`,
   `twitter:card`, `theme-color` and `htmlAttrs: { lang: 'en' }` in `nuxt.config.ts`. Author a
   1200×630 OG image (the couple's names + date over a card film still — `gen-stills.mjs` already
   knows how to pull a frame). Per-route prerendered `<title>` needs the head set at prerender time,
   not in `app.vue` — the simplest honest fix is a `nitro.routeRules` head per chapter route.
   *Verify:* re-read the five built `index.html`s; each must carry its own title and the OG block.
2. **#100 — the bottom chrome.** Give the credit and the sound toggle the same ink decision the top
   nav already makes. `groundIsDark()` returns `false` inside the hero (AUDIT #86) because the
   card's title band is pale — but the **bottom** of the hero is the accent ground on a phone and
   the film on a desktop, so the bottom row needs its own answer, not the top's. Cheapest correct
   version: ink the bottom row in `accentLight` whenever the hero owns the frame.
   *Verify:* re-run the contrast probe at `y=0`; every bottom-chrome row ≥ 4.5 on all four chapters
   at 390×844 and 1440×900.
3. **#104 — one countdown.** One helper, used by both. The dial's `floor` is right for a live clock;
   make the nav read from the same number.

### Round 2 — reading the pages

4. **#101 — the nav and the copy.** Two candidate fixes, and they are not equivalent:
   **(a)** a scrim — a short top gradient in the page's own ground behind the chrome. Cheap,
   universal, and it changes the look. **(b)** give the chapter content a top gutter and let the
   wordmark fade on scroll the way it already fades on the return pull (`homeCue` is the precedent).
   Recommend **(b)** for the wordmark plus **(a)** as a 24px whisper for the corner items.
   *Verify:* the collision probe — target 0 positions with copy under the chrome on /us at 360×640,
   down from 18/26.
5. **#107 — the small type.** Raise `leave-label`, `end-date`, `u-label`, `fmeta`, `dock-note`,
   `hashtag` and the credit to AA. Most are one token change; the credit at 8px/40% wants a size as
   well as an opacity.
6. **#102 — the desktop tagline.** Derive `TXT_Y_DESKTOP` and the ring's idle Y from the frustum the
   way `selectedCarouselY()` already derives the hero's top edge (AUDIT #85), so the gap between the
   tagline's baseline and the top of the cards is a constant fraction of the frame rather than a
   constant in world units. *Verify:* settled screenshots at 1024×768 → 2560×1440, no intersection.

### Round 3 — reach

7. **#105 — keyboard.** `WELCOME`, the wordmark and the sound toggle become `<button>` with
   `aria-expanded` / `aria-pressed`; `Escape` closes the About panel; the close control gets
   `aria-label="Close"`. Small, contained, and it is the difference between the chrome being
   operable and not.
8. **#106 — reduced motion.** Decide the contract first: the honest version is *the scene still
   renders, but the intro lands immediately, the idle spin stops, and select/return cut rather than
   scrub*. Then one `calm` flag through `useChapterScene`, the way `WithLove` already does it.
9. **#103 — landscape phone.** At minimum stop the hero title colliding with the wordmark: pick the
   portrait hero proportions whenever the viewport is shorter than ~500px, whatever its width.
10. **#109 — tap targets.** `.reveal-cash` and `.cash-x` to ≥ 44px.

### Round 4 — housekeeping

11. **#108** — delete the four unused SVGs. **#110** — delete the seven orphaned `POPUPS` entries and
    the `marks[]` comments. **#112** — `robots.txt`, a five-line `sitemap.xml`, and decide whether an
    unknown slug should say anything before it redirects.
12. **#111** — ask the couple to open the five oraimo links from Lagos. If they 403 there too, swap
    for reachable product pages.

### Not in the plan, deliberately

Splitting `useChapterScene.js` (2,437 lines). It is the right thing eventually and the wrong thing
six weeks before a wedding: every issue above is a small, verifiable edit, and a refactor underneath
them turns a QA round into a regression hunt.
