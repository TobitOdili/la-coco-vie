## Method

Clean production build (`rm -rf .output && npm run build`, 13 MB) served statically at
`localhost:5099` — never the dev server, because headless renders card textures only on a prod build
and the films never decode headless at all. Chrome via `playwright-core`
(`chromium.launch({ channel: 'chrome' })`), scene hooks through `?debug`.

| probe | what it did |
|---|---|
| **crawl** | 5 routes × 6 viewports (360×640, 390×844, 768×1024, 844×390, 1440×900, 1920×1080) = 30 loads. Per load: page errors, console errors, failed requests, 4xx/5xx, transferred bytes, `<title>`/`lang`/description/OG/canonical, heading list, horizontal overflow + the elements causing it, every `<img>` (natural vs rendered vs DPR, alt, srcset), every link, every interactive box under 44px, focusable count, and a bounded contrast scan |
| **shells** | the five prerendered `index.html` files read directly — what a crawler or a share preview gets before any JavaScript runs |
| **links** | all 9 external URLs in `chapterPages.js`, `curl -L` with a real browser user agent |
| **countdown** | the nav's own string against the true interval to `2026-10-29T12:00+01:00` |
| **keyboard** | 12 × Tab on the homepage and on a chapter, recording what receives focus and whether it has an outline |
| **reduced motion** | whole site under `reducedMotion: 'reduce'` — deck rotation sampled at 3s and 14s, With Love's wall transforms sampled 1.2s apart |
| **contrast (deep)** | every text-bearing leaf on all five routes at 7 scroll positions each, effective colour after `opacity`, against the nearest opaque ancestor background |
| **nav overlap** | /us scrolled in 60%-viewport steps at three sizes, looking for body copy inside the top 64px |
| **flows** | Welcome panel, sound toggle, hover → select → chapter → bottom exit → home, select → top return → home, deck restoration afterwards |
| **touch** | 390×844 with real `Input.dispatchTouchEvent` gestures: EXPLORE tap, then a nine-stroke downward swipe from the top of a chapter |
| **In Frames** | folder open / Escape / open-by-keyboard, reading `.win-title`, `.view-root.on` and the back button's managed `tabindex` |

⚠️ **Two probe bugs found and fixed mid-pass — both would have produced a clean bill of health.**
1. The nav-overlap probe excluded "anything inside a `position: fixed` ancestor" to skip the chrome.
   A chapter page is *itself* `position: fixed; inset: 0`, so that marked the whole page as chrome
   and passed everything. It reported **0/15** while a screenshot of the same scroll position showed
   a quote crossing the wordmark. Fixed by treating only SMALL fixed boxes (< 220px tall) as chrome.
2. The same probe skipped elements with child *elements*, so a `<p>` containing a `<br>` — which is
   most of US's copy — was never examined at all.

Both are the same failure as AUDIT #123's "world x is not screen direction": **the instrument agreed
with itself and disagreed with the screenshot.** When they disagree, the screenshot wins.

## What is healthy

- **30/30 loads: 0 page errors, 0 console errors, 0 failed requests, 0 horizontal overflow**, at
  every size from a 360px phone to 1920. No 4xx or 5xx from the build.
- **Every registry link resolves.** All 9 URLs in `chapterPages.js` return 200 — including the five
  oraimo ones that the last pass filed as 403s (see #111 below).
- **The countdown is right.** On 2026-09-20 the nav reads *39 days to go*; the calendar difference to
  29 October is 39 days (the exact interval is 38.85, so it is counting whole days the way a person
  does).
- **Every interaction path works.** Hover → click → `/with-love` → scroll to the bottom → exit → `/`;
  select → top return → `/` with all eight cards back at their slots (`localY` 0 across the ring);
  Welcome panel opens and closes; sound toggles OFF → ON. Zero errors across the whole sequence.
- **Touch works.** On a 390×844 phone the EXPLORE tap opens `/us`, and a nine-stroke downward swipe
  from the top of the page returns to the deck.
- **In Frames is the best-built page in the site, accessibility included.** Folders are real
  `<button>`s with `aria-label`s and *managed* `tabindex` (−1 when the view is off screen), the
  window's back button the same. Open by mouse, open by Enter from the keyboard, and Escape returns —
  all verified. **This is the model for fixing #105 elsewhere.**
- **The DOM respects reduced motion.** With Love's six sliding bands freeze completely under
  `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` (verified: identical transforms 1.2s apart, against a 71px shift
  in the same interval without it). The loader's font shuffle holds too, and In Frames' film stills.
- **No image is missing an `alt`.**
- **The deck work landed clean.** The dip, the hover pose and draw order, the laurel badge and the
  loader's mixed faces all render correctly at 360, 390, 768, 844×390, 1440, 1920 and 2560 with no
  errors anywhere.

## Findings

### 🔴 #99 — Still open. A shared link has no preview, and every chapter previews as the homepage

Unchanged since 2026-09-15 and confirmed from the shells themselves, which is what a crawler reads:

```
/index.html             <title>Covenant &amp; Uvie — A Love Story in Chapters</title>
/us/index.html          <title>Covenant &amp; Uvie — A Love Story in Chapters</title>
/the-big-day/index.html <title>Covenant &amp; Uvie — A Love Story in Chapters</title>
/in-frames/index.html   <title>Covenant &amp; Uvie — A Love Story in Chapters</title>
/with-love/index.html   <title>Covenant &amp; Uvie — A Love Story in Chapters</title>
```

In the running app the titles are right (`Coco & Uvie — Covenant & Uvie`, etc.) — `app.vue` sets them
on select. Before JavaScript runs there is one title for five URLs, and:

- **no `lang`** on `<html>` (it carries only `data-capo=""`)
- **no `<meta name="description">`**, on any route
- **no Open Graph or Twitter tags at all** — a WhatsApp or iMessage share shows a bare URL
- **no canonical**

This is the highest-value item in the document. A wedding site is shared by link, and this one is
being shared into group chats for the next five weeks.

### 🔴 #100 — Still open. The bottom chrome disappears at the top of a chapter

Photographed this pass at 1440×900 on `/the-big-day`: over the hero's pale wall, **"WITH LOVE, C & U"
at the bottom left is effectively invisible**, and "OFF 🔇" at the bottom right is only slightly
better. Both sit on the WebGL hero, so the ground is the film, and it cannot be read from the DOM —
which is exactly why the measured contrast below (#107) misses them and why the scroll cue beside
them was tuned by eye (AUDIT #66).

⚠️ **The cue itself is fine.** The deep contrast scan reported "read on" at **1.2:1** on two pages;
cropping the pixels shows it reads clearly — light ink with a stacked dark text-shadow outline over a
dark photograph. The DOM walk had climbed past the transparent page to the white body. **Recorded as
a false positive so the next pass does not re-file it.**

### 🟠 #102 — Half fixed by the deck work, and only on desktop

The original complaint — the centre tagline printed across the tops of the two front cards — is
**resolved at every landscape size from 1280 to 2560**: the ring's dip went 25° → 36° (AUDIT #129),

**Still true on a landscape phone** (844×390), where it is part of #103.

### 🟠 #103 — Still open. Landscape phone is unhandled

At 844×390 the deck is small and centred, the tagline overlaps both front cards, the laurel is hidden
behind them, and a deep empty band runs across the bottom third of the frame. The cause is
structural: `fitScale()` pulls the camera back on a short viewport so the whole 3D composition
shrinks, while the chrome is fixed CSS pixels and does not. Everything that is wrong with this size
follows from those two scaling rules disagreeing.

### ✅ #104 — Closed. The countdown is correct

Not reproducible. On 2026-09-20 the nav reads *39 days to go*; midnight-to-midnight to 29 October is
39 days (exact interval 38.85). Counting whole calendar days is also how a person reads it. No
discrepancy between the homepage and The Big Day was observable this pass.

### 🟠 #105 — Still open, and worse than filed

**The homepage has exactly one focusable element.** Twelve presses of Tab reach `a.menu-item «RSVP»`
and nothing else. A chapter page has two (the RSVP pill in the footer, and RSVP in the nav).

Not reachable by keyboard anywhere: **WELCOME** (opens the welcome note), the **sound toggle**, the
**logo / go-home**, and the **cards** — which are canvas, and have no DOM alternative, so a keyboard
visitor cannot open a single chapter.

The cause is markup, and it is three lines in `SiteNav.vue`:

```
15:  <div class="menu-item" @click="$emit('toggle-about')">     ← WELCOME
65:  … @click="$emit('go-home')"                                 ← the wordmark
138: <div class="menu-item pointer-events-auto" @click="$emit('toggle-sound')">  ← sound
```

`<div>` with `@click`: no tab stop, no Enter/Space, no role, no focus ring. In Frames already does
this correctly (see *What is healthy*) — the same treatment applied to the chrome closes most of it.

## Game plan

Ordered by what a guest loses if it is not done, not by effort.

### Round 1 — the link (the only item with a deadline attached to it)

**#99 + the `<h1>` half of #112.** Per-route `<title>`, `description`, `og:*`/`twitter:*` with the
couple's own image, `lang="en"`, and a canonical — set in `nuxt.config.ts` route rules so they are in
the *prerendered shell*, not applied by `app.vue` after hydration. One `<h1>` per page while in
there, and `/with-love`'s `h3` demoted to fit under it. Add `robots.txt` and a five-line sitemap.
**Risk: none to the running site** — it is all head content and one heading level.

### Round 2 — reach (#105, #137)

`SiteNav.vue`'s three `<div @click>` become `<button type="button">`; Escape closes the welcome note;
and the deck gets a DOM alternative — the four chapter names as real links, visually hidden until
focused, so a keyboard visitor can enter a chapter at all. In Frames already shows the pattern.
**Risk: low.** Buttons inside the nav's flex row need their default styles stripped, which is
cosmetic and visible immediately.

### Round 3 — reading the pages (#107, #101, #100, #109)

Raise the floor on the quiet voice: no functional label below ~11px, and no `opacity` under ~0.75 on
type under 14px — starting with the **bank labels** and the credit. Then the nav overlap on /us
(a top mask or a scroll-padding on the chapter scroller), the bottom chrome over the hero (the cue's
outline treatment already solves this problem next to it), and 44px minimum heights on the registry
words, `reveal-cash` and `go-back`.
**Risk: medium** — every one of these is a visible design change, and the quiet voice is deliberate.
Worth showing the couple a before/after of the gift accounts before doing the rest.

### Round 4 — the phone in landscape (#103) and motion (#106)

The composition rules that disagree at 844×390 — `fitScale()` scaling the scene while the chrome
stays in CSS pixels — need to be reconciled once, and #102's remainder falls out of it. Then a
`prefers-reduced-motion` branch in `useChapterScene.js`: no intro spin (place the ring at rest), no
camera parallax, no hover chase, and instant selects.
**Risk: medium-high** — both touch the scene's composition, which is the thing most recently tuned.

### Round 5 — housekeeping (#108, #110, #138, #139)

Keep `cu-p*.svg` out of `public/` (move the sources to `scripts/assets/`); delete `POPUPS` and the
stale comments; a smaller thumbnail step for In Frames; and re-encode `us.mp4` to sit alongside the
other three films rather than three times their size.
**Risk: none**, and it is the only round that can be done while waiting on the couple.

### Not in the plan, deliberately

The 774 KB grain tile (#118 — resample the original, never replace it, and judge it full-screen);
the theme track's size; and the 1600 device-px tagline crossover. All three are understood, all three
are deliberate, and all three have bitten before when treated as free wins.
